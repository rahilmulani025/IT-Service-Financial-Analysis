## 📌 Project Summary

This project analyzes the **financial and operational impact** of onboarding new clients in a subscription-based IT services company.

The company is considering hiring **6 additional developers** after adding 3 new clients.

This case study evaluates:

✔ Revenue growth
✔ Cost impact
✔ Profit margin changes
✔ Developer capacity utilization
✔ Hiring justification using data-driven modeling

---

## 🏢 Business Context

### 🔹 Current Setup

* 10 Existing Clients
* Each project requires:

  * 👨‍💻 3 Developers
  * 🗓 3 Working Days

### 🔹 Monthly Cost Structure

| Expense Category       | Amount (₹)    |
| ---------------------- | ------------- |
| Developer Salaries     | 20,00,000     |
| EMI Obligations        | 5,00,000      |
| Operational Costs      | 11,00,000     |
| **Total Monthly Cost** | **36,00,000** |

---

## 💰 Revenue Analysis

### 📍 Existing Client Revenue

Total Monthly Revenue = **₹68,06,938**

### 📍 After Onboarding 3 New Clients

Projected Monthly Revenue = **₹83,89,340**

### 📈 Revenue Growth

Increase = **₹15,82,402**

---

## 💸 Cost & Profit Analysis

### 🔹 Scenario Comparison

| Metric        | Current    | After Hiring 6 Devs |
| ------------- | ---------- | ------------------- |
| Revenue       | ₹68,06,938 | ₹83,89,340          |
| Cost          | ₹36,00,000 | ₹48,00,000          |
| Profit        | ₹32,06,938 | ₹35,89,340          |
| Profit Margin | 47.1%      | 42.8%               |

📌 **Key Insight:**
While profit increases in absolute terms, profit margin declines due to higher fixed salary costs.

---

## ⚙️ Operational Capacity Analysis

### 🔹 Workload Calculation

Each Client =
3 Developers × 3 Days = **9 Developer-Days**

| Scenario   | Required Developer-Days |
| ---------- | ----------------------- |
| 10 Clients | 90 Dev-Days             |
| 13 Clients | 117 Dev-Days            |

---

### 🔹 Current Team Capacity

* 10 Developers
* 22 Working Days / Month

Total Capacity =
10 × 22 = **220 Dev-Days**

### 📊 Utilization Rate

| Scenario            | Utilization |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Current             | 41%         |
| After 3 New Clients | 53%         |

🚨 **Insight:**
Even after onboarding new clients, existing team capacity is sufficient.

---

## 📉 Hiring Scenario Simulation (Power BI Model)

A dynamic hiring simulation was created using DAX to evaluate:

* 📊 Profit vs Number of Developers
* 📊 Profit Margin vs Hiring
* 📊 Utilization vs Hiring
* 📊 Cost Structure Impact

### 🔍 Key Finding:

Hiring 6 developers:

* ❌ Reduces utilization to ~33%
* ❌ Compresses profit margin by ~4%
* ❌ Increases fixed cost risk

---

## 🎯 Final Recommendation

Hiring 6 additional developers is **NOT financially justified** at this stage.

### ✅ Recommended Strategy:

* Do not hire immediately
* Monitor utilization for 2–3 months
* Consider phased hiring (1–2 developers) only if utilization exceeds 75%
* Reassess based on future client acquisition
